Future Interns Data Science & Analytics – Task 3
Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis
📌 Objective
The objective of this task is to analyze marketing funnel performance and understand how users move through different stages:

Visitors → Leads → Customers

The analysis focuses on conversion rates, funnel drop-offs, channel performance, monthly trends, and recommendations to improve conversions.

📊 Dataset
A small simulated marketing funnel dataset was created for this analysis.

The dataset contains:

Date/Month
Marketing Channel
Visitors
Leads
Customers
The dataset covers four months and three marketing channels:

Google Ads
Instagram
Facebook
🛠️ Tool Used
Microsoft Excel
📈 Analysis Performed
1. Traffic-to-Lead Conversion
Traffic-to-Lead Conversion was calculated using:

Leads ÷ Visitors × 100

Overall Traffic-to-Lead Conversion: 12.84%

2. Lead-to-Customer Conversion
Lead-to-Customer Conversion was calculated using:

Customers ÷ Leads × 100

Overall Lead-to-Customer Conversion: 24.80%

3. Funnel Drop-off Analysis
The major funnel stages analyzed were:

Visitors → Leads → Customers

Overall drop-offs:

Visitor-to-Lead Drop-off: 10,372
Lead-to-Customer Drop-off: 1,149
4. Channel Performance
The three marketing channels were compared based on visitors, leads, and customers.

Channel	Visitors	Leads	Customers	Lead-to-Customer
Google Ads	4,900	635	175	27.56%
Instagram	3,800	509	115	22.59%
Facebook	3,200	384	89	23.18%
5. Monthly Performance
Month	Visitors	Leads	Customers
January	2,500	304	68
February	2,850	366	90
March	3,250	438	111
April	3,300	420	110
💡 Key Insights
Google Ads generated the highest number of visitors, leads, and customers among the analyzed channels.
Overall Traffic-to-Lead Conversion was 12.84%.
Overall Lead-to-Customer Conversion was 24.80%.
The largest numerical drop-off occurred between visitors and leads.
March recorded the highest number of customers with 111 customers.
Funnel performance varied across both marketing channels and months.
🎯 Recommendations
Improve landing pages and call-to-action messages to increase Visitor-to-Lead conversion.
Analyze successful Google Ads practices and identify approaches that can be tested on other channels.
Improve lead follow-up and engagement to increase Lead-to-Customer conversion.
Monitor funnel performance regularly across channels and months.
Test different campaign messages and landing-page approaches to reduce funnel drop-offs.
